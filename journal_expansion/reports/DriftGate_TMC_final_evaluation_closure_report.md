### 3.1 Pool construction (`src/disjoint_pools.py`)

Per class, a deterministic permutation seeded by the run seed splits test IDs into a **controller/probe pool (20%)** and an **evaluation pool (80%)**, stratified and disjoint. ## 4. Direct non-Main signal comparison (Task 2)

### 4.1 The three signals (identical DV pipeline, no signal-specific tuning)

- **TV (dual-exit):** `mean 0.5·Σ_j |p_client(j) − p_server(j)|` — divergence between the personalized client exit and the generalized server exit.
- **soft (direct, server-only):** `mean(1 − Σ_{j∈Main} p_server(j))` — server probability mass leaving the client's Main classes.
- **hard (direct, server-only):** `mean 1[argmax p_server ∉ Main]` — fraction of probes the server routes outside Main.

Main classes are **training metadata**, never probe/eval labels. All three feed the same temporal/spatial/absolute normalization, EWMA, guard, and λ/Λ mapping with the same frozen constants (`src/signals/library.py::server_nonmain_signals`, `src/controllers/self_calibrating.py`).

### 4.2 Passive signal quality — SVHN (`final_svhn_signal_results.csv`)

Signals for all four candidates are read from **one common neutral backbone per seed** (the entropy-controller run — none of TV/soft/hard shaped it), correlated with the SVHN drift level ρ (a clean 0→0.8 sigmoid ramp; Schedule-A `rho_trace` is identically 0 and cannot be used).

| signal | Spearman(vs ρ) | AUROC(drift-active) |
|---|---|---|
| **hard** | **+0.689** | **0.949** |
| TV | +0.557 | 0.905 |
| soft | +0.275 | 0.789 |
| entropy | **−0.828** | 0.162 |

**Ranking: hard > TV > soft ≫ entropy.** Entropy's correlation is strongly **inverted** (−0.83) — it *decreases* as drift rises (convergence-death), re-confirming why a role-aware, label-free signal is needed at all.

### 4.3 Downstream accuracy (disjoint pools) — `final_signal_comparison.csv`

| block | TV | soft | hard | entropy | fixed | TV−soft | TV−hard |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Schedule A (5 s) | 65.84 | 65.68 | **66.45** | 64.37 | 65.17 | +0.16 (2/5, p=.38) | **−0.61** (1/5, p=.088) |
| mobility (3 s)¹ | 59.21 | 58.82 | 59.40 | 57.21 | 58.58 | +0.39 (3/3, p=.005) | −0.20 (1/3, p=.57) |
| SVHN (5 s) | 79.39 | 80.45 | **80.58** | 76.97 | 80.43 | **−1.06** (0/5, p=.035) | **−1.19** (0/5, p=.023) |

¹ soft/hard mobility have 2 matched seeds vs TV's 3; paired diffs use matched seeds only.

### 4.4 Interpretation (§5.8 rules)

- **hard ≥ TV** on every block (better on Schedule A and SVHN, tie on mobility), and hard is the **best passive signal**. Per the directive's rule ("if the direct signal is higher, do not hide it"), this is reported as a first-class finding.
- **soft is weaker** than both TV and hard (Spearman 0.275) — so *not every* direct signal wins; the strength is specific to the **hard** readout.
- On SVHN, adaptive TV (79.39) is even **slightly below fixed λ0.2 (80.43)**, whereas hard/soft adaptive match fixed — i.e., on SVHN the TV controller's adaptation is marginally miscalibrated relative to the direct readout.
- **This refines, not refutes, the contribution.** hard is itself a *role-separated, server-side, label-free* signal built from the same architectural ingredient (the generalized server head). The defensible paper claim becomes: **the value lies in reading drift off the role-separated server exit; the specific TV-between-exits divergence is not necessary — a direct "server predicts outside Main" readout is at least as good and simpler.** The entropy comparison (label-free inertial signal beats convergence-dying entropy) remains fully intact.

### Bottom line for the paper

The core empirical claims survive the strictest test (disjoint pools): **adaptive-λ DriftGate beats best-fixed and entropy on Schedule A and mobility, with no probe/eval leakage.** Two honest adjustments are required: (i) the gain is **adaptive-λ-driven** (adaptive Λ ≈ 0 independent effect), and (ii) the **specific TV dual-exit divergence is not the key** — a simpler direct server-side "predicts-outside-Main" (`hard`) signal is at least as good and, on SVHN, better. Reframe the contribution around the **role-separated server-side drift signal** rather than the TV computation, and report the direct-signal comparison openly.
