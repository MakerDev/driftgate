# Round 7 gate figure captions

## figA_lambda_home_away

Mean lambda_k of the clients at home (a) and away from home (b) by time of day for the device-level controller with the TV signal (D-TV), with the server-exit non-Main rate (D-SR) and the home/away oracle. Commute mobility, seeds 5 to 7. The dotted line marks fixed lambda 0.4. The device-level controllers hold lambda at 0.425 for the first 25 rounds, while the oracle uses its values from the first round. Few clients are away after 19:00, so the away curves are noisy there.

## figB_accuracy_by_time

Accuracy by time of day for fixed lambda 0.4 (F), the cell-level DriftGate (C), the two device-level controllers and the oracle. Commute mobility, means over seeds 5 to 7. Vertical lines separate the pre-commute, commute, daytime, return and evening periods.
