\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

When users move, the requests of a mobile device alternate between classes that its personalized exit has learned and classes that only the shared edge exit knows. Choosing one exit by confidence follows this change poorly, because the personalized exit is confident on many classes it has never seen. DriftGate combines the two exits instead. It averages their class probabilities after correcting the edge exit's bias against the device's own classes, sets the weight of each exit from the device's recent offloaded requests, and offloads only uncertain requests. Over the first simulated day, DriftGate has the highest mean accuracy of eleven inference rules in eight settings, and in five of them it exceeds every rule that uses the edge for all requests while offloading at most half of the requests. Much of this lead comes from the early phase of training.
